Project Objective
Business Problem: Retailers face a massive surge in demand during festive seasons (like Diwali), making it critical to understand customer purchasing behavior to optimize inventory, marketing budgets, and regional logistics.

Goal: To perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on transactional data to uncover key demographic trends (such as age, gender, and marital status) and high-performing product categories.

Sales Distribution by Gender:
Finding: The analysis clearly indicates that female buyers significantly outnumber male buyers, accounting for the vast majority of total orders and overall sales revenue.
Business Takeaway: Marketing campaigns, festive discount offers, and product recommendations should be heavily tailored toward female shoppers to maximize conversion rates.

Purchasing Behavior Across Age Brackets:
Finding: The 26–35 years age group forms the largest core customer segment, driving the highest volume of purchases across the platform, followed closely by the 35–49 age group.
Business Takeaway: Promotional budgets and targeted ad placements should prioritize young working professionals and young adults aged 26–35, particularly focusing on categories most popular with this demographic.

Customer Segmentation by Marital Status:
Finding: Married women emerge as the highest-spending demographic group in the dataset, exhibiting substantially higher purchasing power and order frequency compared to unmarried buyers.
Business Takeaway: Bundled family offers, home utility discounts, and festive gifting packages targeted at married households will yield the highest return on investment.

Sales Revenue by State:
Finding: The majority of total sales revenue and transaction volume originate from a few high-performing states—predominantly Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka.
Business Takeaway: Supply chain logistics, inventory stocking, and regional warehousing should be optimized in these top-tier states ahead of peak festive seasons to ensure seamless delivery and reduce shipping times.

Top Product Categories by Volume & Revenue:
Finding: Categories such as Food, Clothing & Apparel, and Electronics & Gadgets generate the highest order quantities and total revenue contribution.
Business Takeaway: Inventory management teams should prioritize stocking up on high-demand items in these specific categories well in advance of festive demand spikes.
